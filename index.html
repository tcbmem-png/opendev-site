A contracting methodology

Contracts that work while the project is moving.

Most construction contracts get signed and then ignored until something breaks. OpenDev keeps the contract live. Every dollar lives in a row, every change routes through procedure, every party can see what they're inside of. No smoke.

What OpenDev is.

OpenDev is a methodology for projects that move money and risk across multiple parties over time. The work that produced it: a build-to-suit development with four contracts running concurrently — Owner and Contractor at the center, with Architect and Owner's Representative in coordination roles, and a Tenant signed up to occupy the building once it's built.

Four parties. Fifteen months. Seven million dollars of capital under construction risk. A fifteen-year lease tail behind it. The kind of deal where everyone has standing, no one has full visibility, and the contracts most lawyers write fall apart the first time something unexpected happens.

The contract is the table. Every dollar lives in a row. Every term knows its tier. Nothing changes by accident.

OpenDev classifies every term in every contract by what kind of change it is. Operational items move quickly through ordinary procedure. Structural items route through formal amendment with both parties' signatures. The classification — three hundred terms across four contracts — becomes a live document the parties administer together. We call it the Bridge.

The result: when something happens during construction — a Pay App, a compliance lapse, a Change Order, a substitution that quietly shifts risk — the procedure is already named, the routing is already decided, and every party sees the same truth.
